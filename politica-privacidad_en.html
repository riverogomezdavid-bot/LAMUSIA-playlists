Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Data controller

LAMUSIA is the controller responsible for the personal data collected through this website. You can contact the controller at hola@lamusia.com.

2. What data we collect, and why

Through the "Tell us about your event" form: your name, email, phone (if you provide it), and the details you share about your event. We use this only to reply to your enquiry and, if it goes ahead, to prepare your soundtrack.

Through the request form (accessed via a personal link, either sent directly or through a professional partner we work with): contextual details about the event — average age and home country of the guests, type of atmosphere, time of day, priority song — and the country you, as the requester, are in, which we use to determine the correct Spotify market for your location. This data is used solely to prepare the soundtrack you requested.

3. Legal basis for processing

  • Tell us about your event: your explicit consent, given by checking the corresponding box before submitting the form.
  • Request form: performance of the contractual or pre-contractual relationship already underway with you — this data is necessary to provide the service you requested.

4. How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as our relationship lasts and, afterwards, for the periods legally required to address any potential liabilities. If you write to us through "Tell us about your event" and the conversation doesn't lead anywhere, we keep your data for a maximum of 12 months from the last contact, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes. To provide the service, we use third-party tools that act as data processors: Google (Gmail, Google Sheets and Google Apps Script, to manage emails and the service's database) and Spotify (for the technical creation of the playlist). These providers process data according to their own privacy policies.

6. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability at any time by writing to hola@lamusia.com. If you believe we haven't handled your request properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

7. A technical note about this website's typography

This website loads the Fraunces typeface from Google Fonts' servers, which involves a technical transfer of your IP address to Google when the page loads. This is not used for tracking or advertising purposes.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if our practices or applicable regulations change. The date of the last update appears at the top of this document.
