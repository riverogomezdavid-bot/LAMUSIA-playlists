How we fit in

We don't compete with your DJ. We complete them.

A DJ does something we can't: read the room live and respond in the moment. We do something a DJ isn't there to do: take care of the music during the parts of the event where that's not what's needed.

The DJ

The dance floor. Live reading, energy that rises and falls with the crowd. Irreplaceable there — and we don't try to be.

Lamusia

The ceremony, the cocktail hour, dinner, the time after — or small, intimate events that never had a DJ to begin with. The rest of the day, handled with the same care.
