For professionals

One more thing to offer, with nothing more to manage.

We curate the music for your clients' celebrations, selected and ordered with human judgment — so you can add it to what you already offer, with no operational effort on your part.

Let's talk

How we fit in

With a DJ, we complete it. Without one, we take care of it.

A DJ does something we can't: read the room live and respond in the moment. We take care of everything else: the parts of a celebration where a DJ doesn't fit, and the many private lunches and dinners that never had one.

The DJ

The dance floor. Live reading, energy that rises and falls with the crowd. Irreplaceable there — and we don't try to be.

Lamusia

The ceremony, the cocktail hour, dinner, the lingering after-dinner hours — and the private lunches and dinners that never had a DJ. A tailor-made soundtrack, ordered from start to finish.

Who it's for

For those who look after the experience of clients who seek luxury

You don't need to organize the celebration: it's enough to be there for whoever lives it — through the table, the space or the planning — and to want the music to live up to the rest.

Catering and private chefs

You look after the dining experience, at a grand celebration or at a private lunch or dinner. The music can live up to the menu, without you having to manage it.

Wedding planners

The parts of the wedding when the DJ isn't on, or small, intimate celebrations that never had one — one more detail to offer your clients.

Spaces and venues

One more service for whoever books your space, with no operational cost to you and nothing to coordinate on the day of the celebration.

How it works

Three steps, on your side

01

We talk about your clients

Who you serve and at which celebrations, so we can see how we fit together.

02

You add it to your offer

We give you a personal link for each client: in a few minutes, they tell us what the celebration will be like. For you, no extra effort.

03

We curate

We select and order every song as a musical journey, from start to finish, and deliver it ready: all that's left is to press play.
