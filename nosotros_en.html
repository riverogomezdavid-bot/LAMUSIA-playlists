About us

There's no formula. There's someone listening.

LAMUSIA comes from a simple idea: that the music at an event deserves the same care as any other detail — and that care can't be fully automated, it has to be listened to.

Mission

To turn the music at every private event into a detail as carefully handled as any other — curated with the same judgment and care used to choose the menu, the venue or the flowers, and delivered without the host having to think about it.

Vision

To become the reference for music curation for high-end private events anywhere in the world — combining human judgment with our own learning to anticipate what works, without ever losing the personal touch that made it possible from day one.

Our approach

Three ideas that don't change, request after request.

Listened to, not just generated

Every playlist starts with a broad selection, but none goes out without someone actually listening to it first, song by song.

We complement, we don't compete

We don't replace a DJ on the dance floor — we take care of the music in the moments where a DJ doesn't fit, with the same level of care.

We learn from every event

What works in one playlist carries over to the next — our judgment gets sharper over time, it's never just repeated without thought.
