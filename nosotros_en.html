About us

There's no formula. There's someone listening.

Lamusia comes from a simple idea: that the music at a celebration deserves the same care as any other detail — and that care can't be fully automated, it has to be listened to.

Mission

To turn the music at every private celebration into a detail as carefully handled as any other — curated with the same judgment and attention used to choose the menu, the venue or the flowers, and delivered without the host, or whoever looks after them, having to think about it.

Vision

To be the reference in music curation for high-end private celebrations anywhere in the world — combining human judgment with our own learning to anticipate what works, without ever losing the personal touch that made it possible from day one.

Our approach

Three ideas that don't change, order after order.

Listened to, not just generated

Every playlist starts with a broad selection, but none is delivered until someone truly listens to it, song by song, and orders it as a journey from start to finish.

We complement, we don't compete

We don't replace a DJ on the dance floor — we look after the music where a DJ doesn't fit, and at private lunches and dinners that never had one, with the same level of detail.

We learn from every celebration

What works in one playlist carries over to the next — our judgment sharpens over time; it is never simply repeated without thought.
